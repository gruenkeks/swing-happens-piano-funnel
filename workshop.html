Do you recognize yourself here?

1

You spend a lot of time watching videos about improvisation, scales, voicings, licks and other piano tips online, jumping from one idea to the next.

You find yourself in information-collection mode, stuck in the paralysis of analysis, without really making progress in your playing.

2

You take private lessons, maybe with an excellent pianist, but even there you jump from scales to chords, sight-reading, voicings, tunes, improvisation, comping — without a clear line to follow.

You learn many things, but they don’t necessarily become part of your actual playing.

3

Perhaps you even studied at a music college and were given so much material over 4–5 years that you ended up feeling completely lost.

You know a lot, but you can’t find the common thread that gives you clarity and enables you to constantly improve.

Who is the LEAKS Method for?

  • Everyone who already plays piano beyond the first steps — advanced beginners, intermediate players, serious students and professionals.
  • As a professional musician — jazz, classical or other styles — the exercises develop technique, hearing, time feel and vocabulary.
  • As a beginner in improvisation you get step-by-step instructions for harmony, rhythm, melody and improvisation, without extra noise.

And would you rather learn instead…

  • how to practice with a clear line and a clear goal
  • how to understand and master technical challenges in depth
  • how to develop your time feel and play with greater rhythmic confidence
  • how to improvise melodies that have direction and meaning
  • how to take the things you already know and actually use them in your playing

The good news: even if it sounds too good to be true — it’s easier than you think.

In the Musical Potential Analysis, you see what is holding your playing back, what to focus on next, and how to stop wasting hours jumping from one exercise to another.

Less information. More implementation. Better playing.
